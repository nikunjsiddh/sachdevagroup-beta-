Waste Management

Responsible Practices for a Sustainable Future

  • "We implement a robust waste segregation process to separate materials such as steel, non-ferrous metals, plastics, and hazardous substances efficiently."
  • "Each material is categorized for recycling, reuse, or safe disposal, minimizing landfill contributions."
  • "All hazardous materials, including asbestos, lead-based paints, residual fuels, and PCBs, are carefully removed and handled in accordance with international environmental and safety standards."
  • "Our trained specialists use advanced techniques and tools to manage hazardous substances safely, ensuring zero harm to workers and the environment."
  • "Over 90% of the materials from dismantled ships are recycled, significantly contributing to resource conservation and circular economy goals."
  • "Reusable components such as machinery, spare parts, and fittings are refurbished and repurposed wherever possible."
  • "We employ advanced containment systems and impermeable surfaces to prevent soil and water contamination during waste handling."
  • "Effluent treatment plants are used to treat and recycle wastewater generated during operations." (GEPIL)
