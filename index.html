About Us

Our Company Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breaking Unit) LLP and Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd. is involved in the business since ship breaking activity started at Alang in 1983.

Ship breaking business was first carried out by Our Group under the name of Madhav Industrial Corporation till 1997. In 1997, we started the business under the name Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship breakers) and till date 36 ships have been Recycled. To further expand the business we established another unit in the year 1998 under the name of Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd. and till date 35 ships have been Recycled.

Sachdeva Group

Since our inception in 1983, SACHDEVA GROUP has been a pioneer in the ship recycling industry, committed to sustainable practices and innovative solutions. Our journey began with a vision to transform the ship recycling sector, ensuring it aligns with environmental standards and safety regulations.

Early Years:

Our founder, established SACHDEVA GROUP with a single ship recycling yard, driven by a passion for environmental stewardship and a deep understanding of the maritime industry. The early years were marked by relentless dedication to building a team of skilled professionals and investing in state-of-the-art facilities.

Growth and Expansion:

By the early 2000s, our commitment to quality and sustainability earned us a reputation as a trusted partner in the global ship recycling market. We expanded our operations and added a unit in the name of JAI JAGDISH SHIP BREAKERS, our yards are equipped with advanced technologies to handle ships of all sizes. This period also saw the implementation of rigorous health, safety, and environmental (HSE) standards, setting a benchmark for the industry.

Innovation and Compliance:

SACHDEVA GROUP continuously adapted and improved our processes in response to evolving international regulations, such as the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships. We integrated cutting-edge technologies to enhance efficiency, reduce environmental impact, and ensure the highest safety standards for our workforce.

Community and Workforce:

We believe that our success is deeply intertwined with the well-being of our employees and the communities we operate in. Over the years, we have invested in comprehensive training programs, ensuring our workforce is skilled, safe, and motivated.

Looking Forward:

Today, Sachdeva Group stands as a leader in the ship recycling industry, recognized for our unwavering commitment to sustainability, safety, and innovation. As we look to the future, we remain dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible in ship recycling, striving to leave a positive legacy for generations to come.
