The Yard Process

From arrival to re-rolling mill

Every vessel moves through four controlled stages, each with its own inspection gate, waste stream and record.

Beaching & Survey

Vessel arrival, gas-free certification and a full inventory of hazardous materials before any cutting begins.

Controlled Dismantling

Block-by-block cutting on impermeable flooring, with cranes and winches rated to the load profile of each section.

Waste Segregation

Asbestos, oils, PCBs and e-waste separated into licensed streams and routed to authorised downstream facilities.

Recovery & Reuse

Re-rollable plate, machinery and fittings graded, weighed and released back into the Indian steel economy.

Our Journey

A legacy built plate by plate

Since our inception in 1983, Sachdeva Group has been a pioneer in the ship recycling industry — committed to sustainable practice and to raising the benchmark of the sector.

  • 1983

    The Early Years

    Our founder established the group with a single recycling yard at Alang, driven by environmental stewardship and a deep understanding of the maritime trade. The early years went into building a skilled team and investing in facilities.

  • 1997 – 1998

    Growth & Expansion

    Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breakers) formalised the operation, and Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd. followed a year later — doubling capacity and equipping both yards to handle ships of all sizes.

  • 2000s

    Innovation & Compliance

    Processes were continuously adapted to evolving international regulation, including the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships, with rigorous HSE standards throughout.

  • Today

    Community & Workforce

    Comprehensive training programmes keep our workforce skilled, safe and motivated — because our success is inseparable from the well-being of our people and the communities around the yard.

  • Looking Forward

    The Next Decade

    We remain dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what is possible in ship recycling, striving to leave a positive legacy for the generations that follow.
