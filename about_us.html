About Sachdeva Group

The Sachdeva Group, through its companies Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breaking Unit) LLP and Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd., has been a cornerstone in the ship recycling industry since its inception in Alang, Gujarat, in 1983.

Who We Are

Sachdeva Group Profile

The Group operates on approximately 4,950 square meters of dedicated land, with the capacity to process over 100,000 lightweight tons of ships annually.

Our journey began under the name Madhav Industrial Corporation, which led the way in shipbreaking until 1997. In that year, we rebranded to Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breakers) and have successfully recycled 37 ships. To meet growing demand and further expand our operations, we established Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers Pvt. Ltd. in 1998, which has since recycled 36 ships.

By combining decades of experience with a commitment to sustainable practices, Sachdeva Group continues to lead the way in responsible ship recycling.

Our Heritage

Four decades, one coastline

From a single yard at Alang to two dedicated recycling units working to the same standard.

  1. 1983

    Madhav Industrial Corporation

    The family enters ship recycling in the first year of ship-breaking activity at Alang, operating under the name Madhav Industrial Corporation.

  2. 1997

    Sachdeva Steel Products

    The operation is rebranded to Sachdeva Steel Products (Ship Breakers), carrying the original yard forward under the group's own name.

  3. 1998

    Jai Jagdish Ship Breakers

    A second company is established to meet growing demand, doubling capacity and equipping both yards to handle vessels of all classes.

  4. Today

    73 vessels recycled

    Two units, 4,950 square meters of dedicated land and the capacity to process over 100,000 lightweight tons of shipping every year.

What Drives The Yard

Where we are going, and how we get there

01

Vision

To be a global leader in sustainable ship recycling, dedicated to minimizing environmental impact, ensuring the highest standards of safety and health, and creating value through innovation and excellence.

We aim to set new benchmarks in green ship recycling by integrating advanced technologies, fostering a culture of responsibility and integrity, and contributing to a circular economy for a cleaner, safer, and more sustainable future.

02

Mission

Our mission is to lead the ship recycling industry by delivering safe, sustainable, and environmentally responsible solutions.

We are committed to minimizing environmental impact, ensuring the highest standards of safety for our workforce, and adhering to international regulations and best practices. Through innovation, integrity, and a dedication to excellence, we aim to recycle ships responsibly, maximize resource recovery, and contribute to a greener, more sustainable future for the maritime industry.
